# VoiceBill Pro short video scripts

Vertical video (9:16) for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Film on your phone, ideally in or next to a work truck. Keep each one under 30 seconds. Put the caption text on screen; most people watch muted.

## 1. "The 20-second invoice" (main demo)
| Time | Shot | On-screen text | You say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2s | You in the truck, holding the phone | **Invoice in 20 seconds?** | "Watch me do an invoice before I leave the driveway." |
| 2-10s | Screen recording: tap 🎙 Speak, talk | **Just talk** | "Replaced a kitchen faucet for Maria. Two hours labor. Faucet one-forty, supply lines eighteen." |
| 10-15s | Screen recording: tap Make invoice, review screen appears | **Labor, parts, tax: done** | "Labor, parts, tax. Done." |
| 15-22s | Tap Email to customer, show the sent message | **Sent ✅** | "And it's in her inbox." |
| 22-28s | Back to you | **Free: voicebillpro.com** | "Free for 5 invoices a month. Link in bio." |

## 2. "Stop doing invoices at night"

## 3. "It won't guess your prices"
| Time | Shot | On-screen text | You say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | You to camera | **My app refuses to guess** | "Forgot to say a price? It won't make one up." |
| 3-12s | Say a note without a price for one part | | "Swapped the thermostat and a filter, one hour." |
| 12-20s | Show the red flagged line, type the price | **Missing price = red** | "It flags it red so you fill it in. No surprises for the customer." |
| 20-26s | Total updates | **voicebillpro.com** | "Free for 5 invoices a month." |

## 4. "Plumber/electrician reacts" (once you have a tester)
Film a real contractor trying it for the first time. Ask: "How long does an invoice usually take you?" Then let them use it on camera. Real reactions beat any script.
